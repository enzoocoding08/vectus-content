The perfect study strategy is not about studying longer.

It is a simple feedback loop:

1. Retrieve the idea from memory.
2. Check exactly what you missed.
3. Repair only the weak link.
4. Repeat before you forget.

Rereading makes information feel familiar. Active recall makes it usable.

Save this strategy for your next study session.

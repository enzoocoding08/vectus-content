Pretty notes are overrated.

Not because notes are useless. They can help you organize information, understand a new concept, and create a useful reference.

But making notes is not the same as learning.

Rereading creates familiarity. Learning requires you to retrieve the idea without seeing it.

Try this:
- Close your notes
- Explain the topic from memory
- Answer questions without help
- Check exactly what you missed
- Repair those gaps

Do not study until you have seen everything.
Study until you can recall it without seeing it.

Be honest: How much of your study time is actually just making notes?

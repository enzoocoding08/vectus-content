# Brief

- Audience problem: Learners repeat one problem type in a block, become fluent at the steps, then freeze when an exam mixes methods.
- Hook: Repeating one problem type feels efficient. It may be teaching you to follow a pattern, not choose a method.
- Learning principle / aha: Interleaving related problem types adds method selection to practice; the learner must notice cues and decide which approach fits.
- Visual proof: A blocked sequence (AAAA BBBB CCCC) becomes a mixed sequence (A C B A B C), followed by a simple explain-your-choice check.
- Product solution: No direct product claim; teach an evidence-aligned practice structure Vectus Lern can support.
- CTA: Save this mixed-practice recipe for your next problem set.

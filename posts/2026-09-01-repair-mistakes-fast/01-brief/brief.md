# Brief

- Audience problem: Learners mark an answer wrong, read the solution, and move on without fixing the cause.
- Hook: You got it wrong. Good. Now do this before your brain wastes the mistake.
- Learning principle / aha: An error becomes useful when the learner diagnoses it, repairs the smallest gap, and retrieves the answer again.
- Visual proof: A five-minute loop turns one red X into a named cause, a targeted repair, and a clean retest.
- Product solution: No direct product claim; demonstrate the evidence-aligned learning behavior Vectus Lern is designed to support.
- CTA: Save this five-minute mistake repair loop.
- Content pillar: mistakes as learning signals / targeted gap repair
- Format: 7-slide, 1080x1080 Instagram carousel
- Platforms: Instagram

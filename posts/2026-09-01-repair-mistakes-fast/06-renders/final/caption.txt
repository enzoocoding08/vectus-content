Getting an answer wrong is not automatically productive. What you do next decides whether the mistake becomes feedback or gets repeated.

Use this five-minute repair loop:

1. Name the cause: missing knowledge, wrong method, misread prompt, or careless execution.
2. Repair only that gap.
3. Close the solution and redo the problem from a blank page.
4. Explain why the corrected step works.
5. Retest with a similar question later.

Reading the correction can feel clear while the answer is still in front of you. A clean retry shows whether the learning changed.

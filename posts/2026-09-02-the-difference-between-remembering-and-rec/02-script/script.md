## Slide 1

Remembering vs Recognizing bold

One lets you reproduce knowledge; the other tells you it seems familiar. Which matters for real skills?

## Slide 2

What is remembering? plain

Active retrieval of facts or steps without cues. You reconstruct answers from memory.

## Slide 3

What is recognizing? plain

Identifying correct information when it's presented. Often based on familiarity, not full retrieval.

## Slide 4

Why remembering helps more plain

Retrieval strengthens memory, reveals gaps, and improves transfer to new problems.

## Slide 5

When recognition is enough plain

Quick scans, multiple-choice elimination, or initial exposure can use recognition efficiently.

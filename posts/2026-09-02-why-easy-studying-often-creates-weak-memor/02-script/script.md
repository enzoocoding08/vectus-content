## Slide 1

Why 'Easy' Study Fails Memory #ff4da6

Fluency after rereading or cramming masks weak, short‑lived memory. Effortful retrieval makes learning stick.

## Slide 2

Fluency is a familiarity trick #ffd83d

Rereading boosts processing ease but not recall strength. Familiarity feels like knowing but fails when you must produce answers.

## Slide 3

Retrieval beats re-exposure #37f28a

Actively trying to recall information strengthens memory traces far more than passive re‑exposure does.

## Slide 4

Cramming gives short gains #ff4da6

Massed practice inflates session performance but causes rapid forgetting. Spacing forces retrieval across time and improves retention.

## Slide 5

Context limits future cues #ffd83d

Studying in only one format ties memory to that context. Use different prompts and formats to broaden retrieval cues.

## Slide 6

Aim for desirable difficulty #37f28a

Make practice slightly challenging but attainable: closed‑book recall, short summaries, or brief self‑quizzes before checking answers.

## Slide 7

Memorable Takeaway #ff4da6

Easy studying feels productive but builds fragile memory. Prioritize spaced, effortful retrieval with corrective feedback. What's one study habit you'll change?

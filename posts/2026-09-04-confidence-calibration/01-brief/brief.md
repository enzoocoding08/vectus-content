# Brief

- Audience problem: Students judge readiness by how familiar or confident a topic feels, then discover under test conditions that confidence and performance do not match.
- Hook: Feeling confident is not proof you know it.
- Learning principle / aha: Calibration improves when learners predict confidence before a closed-book answer, score the answer, and compare the two rather than relying on a feeling alone.
- Visual proof: A simple calibration grid separates accurate confidence from overconfidence and uncertainty, then turns each result into a next action.
- Product solution: No direct product claim; teach a measurement loop aligned with Vectus Lern's focus on retrieval and targeted gap repair.
- CTA: Ask which topic the reader may be most confidently wrong about.
- Content pillar: learning science / metacognition / exam preparation
- Format: 7-slide, 1080x1080 Instagram carousel
- Platforms: Instagram

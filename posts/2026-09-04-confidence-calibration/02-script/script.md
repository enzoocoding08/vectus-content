# Script and beats

## HOOK

Feeling confident is not proof you know it.

## PROBLEM

Familiar notes and smooth explanations can make an answer feel available while the source is still doing the work.

## EXPLANATION

Before checking, answer one question closed-book and record your confidence: low, medium, or high. Then score what you actually produced.

## AHA

Compare confidence with performance. High confidence plus a weak answer exposes a dangerous blind spot. A strong answer with low confidence shows knowledge that needs reinforcement, not relearning from zero.

## NUANCE

Confidence is not useless. It becomes useful when it is calibrated against observable performance across several questions, not trusted by itself.

## PRODUCT

Use the loop: predict, retrieve, score, adjust. Revisit confidently wrong answers first; practise uncertain correct answers later; move on from consistently accurate ones.

## CTA

Which topic are you most confidently wrong about?

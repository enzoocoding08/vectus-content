Confidence can help you judge your learning—but only after you test whether it matches performance.

Try this calibration loop:

1. Choose one question before opening your notes.
2. Predict your confidence: low, medium, or high.
3. Answer from memory.
4. Score the answer against clear criteria.
5. Compare the feeling with the result.

Prioritize answers that were confidently wrong. Reinforce answers that were correct but uncertain. Move on only when strong performance repeats.

Do this across several questions. One result can be noisy; a pattern is much more useful.

# Brief

- Audience problem: Students mistake moving quickly through familiar flashcards for successful retrieval.
- Hook: Flashcards are not a study method.
- Angle: The card is only a tool; prompt quality and retrieval behavior determine whether it creates learning.
- Nuance: Flashcards are excellent for repeatable, spaced retrieval when learners answer before revealing.
- Goal: Saves, comments, and useful debate through Study Court.
- CTA: What makes a flashcard useful to you?

# Carousel script

1. Hook: Flashcards are not a study method.
2. Accusation: Familiar prompts can produce recognition instead of recall.
3. Evidence: Delaying the reveal turns the card into retrieval practice.
4. Limitation: Shallow fact pairs can omit relationships and reasoning.
5. Better approach: Ask for explanations, examples, comparisons, or next steps.
6. Defense: Flashcards are useful for repeatable, spaced retrieval.
7. Verdict: Measure what you can retrieve and use, not cards reviewed.
8. Jury: Overrated or just misused?

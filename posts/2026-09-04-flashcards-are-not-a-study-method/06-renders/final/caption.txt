Flashcards do not create active recall by themselves.

If you flip the card as soon as the answer feels familiar, you may only be testing recognition. Pause first. Produce the answer. Explain it. Apply it. Then reveal the back and repair what was missing.

Flashcards can be excellent for spaced retrieval—but the quality of the prompt and the honesty of the attempt matter more than the number of cards reviewed.
